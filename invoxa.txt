Design and develop a modern, scalable, multi-user business management system focused on invoice and quotation generation, tracking, and management for small and medium-sized businesses. The platform should allow business owners and their teams to create, edit, manage, and monitor quotations and invoices in real time through a secure and user-friendly interface.

### Core Features

* User authentication and role-based access control (Admin, Manager, Staff, Accountant, Viewer)
* Multi-tenant architecture so multiple businesses can use the same platform independently
* Dashboard with analytics and financial summaries
* Invoice creation, editing, duplication, and PDF export
* Quotation generation with approval and conversion into invoices
* Real-time invoice tracking (Paid, Pending, Overdue, Draft, Cancelled)
* Automatic invoice numbering and tax calculations
* Customer/client management system
* Product and service catalog management
* Payment tracking and transaction history
* Email and WhatsApp invoice sharing
* Search, filtering, and reporting tools
* Notifications and reminders for overdue invoices
* Multi-currency and multi-language support
* Responsive web and mobile-friendly design

### Advanced Features

* Cloud-based synchronization
* Audit logs to track edits and activities
* Team collaboration and permissions
* Recurring invoices and subscription billing
* Integration with payment gateways
* Financial reports and export to Excel/PDF
* AI-powered insights for revenue trends and unpaid invoices

### Technical Requirements

* Use a modern frontend framework such as React or Next.js
* Backend with Node.js, Express, or NestJS
* PostgreSQL for relational database management
* REST API or GraphQL architecture
* JWT/OAuth authentication
* Scalable cloud-ready deployment architecture
* Clean UI/UX with professional dashboard components
* Modular and maintainable codebase following senior engineering standards

### Goal

The system should help business owners efficiently manage quotations and invoices, improve financial tracking, reduce manual work, and enable multiple users within a company to collaborate securely and effectively on daily business operations.
